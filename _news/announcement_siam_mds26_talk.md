Gave an invited talk titled <em>Continuity and Universal Approximation of the Conditioning Operator for Probability Densities</em> in the mini-symposium <a href="https://meetings.siam.org/sess/dsp_programsess.cfm?SESSIONCODE=89641" target="_blank"><em>Blending Data Assimilation and Control</em></a> at the <a href="https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/mds26/" target="_blank">SIAM Conference on Mathematics of Data Science (MDS26)</a>, discussing how neural operators can amortize probabilistic conditioning. The talk is based on our preprint <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06873" target="_blank"><em>One Operator for Many Densities: Amortized Approximation of Conditioning by Neural Operators</em></a>.
